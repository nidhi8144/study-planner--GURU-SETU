**2-MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT — GURUSETU**



**0:00–0:15 | Opening**

**“Hello everyone. This is GuruSetu, my Teachers’ Day Hackathon 2026 project. The idea is simple: turn a learner’s broad goal into clear daily actions, practice and measurable progress.”**



**0:15–0:35 | Problem**

**“Many learners know what exam or subject they want to prepare for, but the difficult part is deciding what to study today, how much time to spend, and how to work on weak areas.”**



**0:35–1:00 | Solution**

**“GuruSetu captures four inputs: learning goal, subject, hours per day and weak area. When I generate the plan, it breaks the goal into seven actions: foundation, focused practice, weak-area repair, active recall, mixed practice, revision and reflection.”**



**1:00–1:25 | Live Demo**

**“I can mark each day as completed. The progress indicator immediately updates. The app also provides five quick MCQs so learning is not just passive reading. I can submit the answers and instantly see my score and whether I should revise.”**



**1:25–1:45 | Technology \& Impact**

**“The prototype is built using HTML, CSS and JavaScript with LocalStorage, so it is lightweight and works without an API key. The goal is to make structured learning accessible, practical and easy to start.”**



**1:45–2:00 | Future Scope \& Close**

**“Next, GuruSetu can add AI-generated plans, teacher dashboards, adaptive quizzes, analytics and multilingual support. GuruSetu represents the hackathon theme: From Learning to Building. Thank you.”**
