**From Learning to Building — I’m excited to present GuruSetu 🚀**



**For the SarlaYash Teachers’ Day Hackathon 2026, I built GuruSetu — a simple learning companion that turns a learner’s goal, available study time and weak area into a practical 7-day action plan.**



**What it includes:**

**• Personalized study planning**

**• Weak-area focused revision**

**• Quick 5-question practice**

**• Progress tracking**

**• Responsive, easy-to-use interface**



**The biggest learning for me was that a useful solution does not have to start with complexity. It starts by understanding a real learner problem and turning that problem into a working prototype.**



**Grateful to all teachers, mentors and the SarlaYash community for encouraging us to learn, build and share.**
